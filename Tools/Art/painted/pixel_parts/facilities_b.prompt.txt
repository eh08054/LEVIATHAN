Crisp, clean pixel art for a calm city-management game, seen from a 3/4 top-down view (as in classic top-down pixel RPGs and city sims: we see roofs from above and the front facades facing us, no side faces, no isometric diamond). Every art pixel is a square block of exactly 4 x 4 image pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, a limited palette of about 32 colours. Near future, year 2041, calm and believable, NOT cyberpunk: white and pale grey buildings with softly rounded corners, deep terraces with plants, light warm wood accents, blue-cyan glass, thin solar canopies, rooftop gardens, an occasional thin cyan light strip. Soft daylight from the upper left, short shadows falling down-right. Gentle, slightly desaturated colours.

This is a SPRITE SHEET on a fully transparent background: separate pieces laid out in a tidy grid with wide empty transparent gaps between them, nothing touching, no labels, no text, no frame.

THREE large city-facility sprites, each standing on its own small plot: (1) a calm modern hospital with a white wing, a glass entrance hall and a small rooftop helipad; (2) a neighbourhood security station, white and pale blue, low and friendly, with two parked patrol pods; (3) a closed construction site for a future facility behind a neat tall fence wrapped in a pale cyan mesh, a crane and a half-built frame inside.
