Two reference images: the first is a flat layout sketch that only says WHERE each district sits (do not copy its colors, labels or shapes); the second is the current painting, used only for mood, palette and each district's landmark. Paint a new image of the same fictional cyberpunk megacity at night.

GROUND AND PERSPECTIVE (the most important part): every district stands directly on the city's ground at street level. No raised platforms, no plinths, no decks, no visible side walls, nothing floating. A continuous flat ground plane must be clearly readable across the whole image: wide dark streets and boulevards with glowing lane lines, plazas and pavement visible between all districts, so the eye can measure height and distance. Clear height order: the AI operations tower in the center is by far the tallest; each district's landmark buildings are second; the ordinary city fabric between districts is low to mid-rise, dense but clearly lower than the districts' landmarks; only near the image's outer edges may a few tall towers rise. Buildings must not hide the districts behind them. Consistent perspective and scale everywhere, one light direction, wet reflective ground.

DISTRICTS (positions from the sketch, landmarks from the second image): center the AI operations tower with a ring of cyan light at its base; just below it a round plaza with a holographic fountain ringed by a low curved building with screens (warm gold); left-middle a neon market quarter (magenta); bottom-left residential towers with green roof gardens (warm windows); bottom-right a school campus with a glowing sports field and a geodesic dome (violet); right-middle a security precinct with watch towers and searchlights (blue); right-upper a white medical complex with a helipad (mint green); top-center a research campus with glass domes and server spires (ice blue); top-left a power plant with glowing reactor domes (orange); left a logistics hub with cargo modules and drone pads (amber); far top-right a construction pit with cranes (dim). Each district is a compact, clearly bounded block with a crisp outer edge at ground level (a street or a low curb all around it), roughly the same footprint size, separated from its neighbors by open streets, so a thin glowing outline could later be drawn around it on the ground.

FOR THE GAME UI: this is the background of a strategy game's city map. Glowing outlines, soft area highlights and darkening of the unselected city will be drawn over it, and translucent dark-navy UI panels with cyan accents will cover the left and right thirds. So: districts are clearly brighter and more colorful than the city around them; the ordinary city is calm, dark navy and desaturated so overlays read on top of it; no glowing lines that look like borders, no neon outlines, no holographic UI, no data lines, no text, letters, logos or icons painted into the image; keep the important districts within the central area and the outer edges calmer. Palette: deep navy, with cyan, magenta, amber, violet and mint lights only in the districts. Mood: rainy night, light haze, cinematic semi-realistic concept art, crisp detail, no people in close-up.
CAMERA: steeper bird's-eye view looking down at about 65 degrees, close to a map, buildings still read as 3D but the street grid and every district's ground footprint are fully visible.
